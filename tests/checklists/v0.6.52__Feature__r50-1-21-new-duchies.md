# Human Test Checklist: the duchies 1.21 added, before the game converts a save

**Target version:** 0.6.52
**Ships with commit:** v0.6.52 (TBD at writing)
**Companion automated tests:** `tests/test_transplant.py::test_r50_...` (adds a new duchy that has counties and a known kingdom; skips a titular duchy and one whose kingdom the save lacks; moves the county; unheld; the `l` answer adds none). `tests/test_update.py` (R50 first in the `to_1_21` pack). The S4 move it reuses: `test_s4_...`.

## What this is

The game's 1.21 conversion never creates a duchy your 1.20 save lacks. So the duchies 1.21 split out of older ones, Artois, Liège, Passau, Upper Lombardy and seven more, are missing from a converted campaign, and they have no market. R50 adds each one that has counties in your save, before your first load in 1.21. Each is unheld, as in a new game, and its counties are moved in. The game then builds its market at the conversion. This checklist is for a person with a 1.20 save and 1.21. The `duchies` question itself is covered by `v0.6.51__Feature__new-duchies-get-their-counties.md`.

## Prerequisites

1. The tool at 0.6.52, a 1.20 save (`camp.ck3`), a 1.21 reference save (`ref21.ck3`, a new 1.21 game on day one), and the 1.21 game.
2. **Isolation:** outputs to a scratch folder; any save you load named `MIGRATE_TEST__...`. Your own saves are never written.

## High-Value Verification (~5 minutes)

### HV.1: the duchies are added

- [ ] **Steps:** `ck3update update camp.ck3 --reference ref21.ck3` (a 1.21 game folder), writing to scratch.
- [ ] **Expected:** R50's report lists `new_duchies` (the founding campaign: 11) and `counties_to_new_duchies`. `inserted` shows only titles and coats of arms, one each per duchy.
- [ ] **Why this matters:** anything else inserted would be content you did not ask for.

### HV.2: they get markets in 1.21

- [ ] **Steps:** load the output in 1.21 (the first load converts it), save, and count markets (the trade map, or a save tool).
- [ ] **Expected:** as many markets as a new 1.21 game on the same map: 922 on the founding campaign (911 without R50).
- [ ] **Why this matters:** the markets are the point.

### HV.3: nobody is given the new duchies

- [ ] **Steps:** in 1.21, open Passau (or another listed duchy).
- [ ] **Expected:** no holder; its de jure counties are listed; the old duchy (Bavaria for Passau) no longer lists them.
- [ ] **Why this matters:** a carried campaign's rulers keep exactly the titles they had.

### HV.4: the purist answer adds none

- [ ] **Steps:** the same update with `--auto duchies=l` (or `all=newgame`).
- [ ] **Expected:** R50 reports the duchies with `answer: left ...`, and none is written.
- [ ] **Why this matters:** it is the "as the game intended" mode.

## Section 1: what is left to the game

### 1.1 counties 1.21 adds itself

- [ ] **Steps:** after HV.2, look at a new duchy whose counties were not all in your 1.20 save (Liège, Passau on the founding campaign).
- [ ] **Expected:** note where the game put the counties it created at the conversion: inside the new duchy, or elsewhere. Report either way. The tool does not write those counties.
